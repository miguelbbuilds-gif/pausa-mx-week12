# Content plan — PAUSA MX Week 15

Promise: “Antes de comprar, compara qué cambia en tu dinero disponible con los datos que ingreses.”

CTA: “Prueba una compra.”

Claim classes used here: VERIFIED, ASSUMPTION, DEMO, UNKNOWN.

No concept below is a finished asset. The three filesets in `public/week15/` are the finished assets. They illustrate C1, C5, and C3. C2, C4, and C6 remain concepts.

## Demo ledger

Shared obligations, DEMO:

- Disponible $18,000
- Esenciales $9,000
- Comprometidos $2,500
- Después de esas obligaciones: $6,500

Phone, cash, DEMO: precio $12,000. Comprar ahora deja −$5,500 hoy. Esperar deja $6,500 hoy.

Headphones, cash, DEMO: precio $1,800. Quedarían $4,700 hoy. That remainder is arithmetic, not affordability.

Written plan, DEMO: enganche $2,000 + 12 × $1,100 = $15,200. Difference versus the $12,000 cash price: $3,200. After the down payment, $4,500 would remain today in this example. Future payments are not matched to future income.

## Series 1 — Antes de comprar

Everyday purchases, demo data, before paying. The series does not grade the purchase.

### C1 — El celular de $12,000

- Hook: Antes de comprar un celular de $12,000…
- Audience: adult in Mexico about to make a discretionary purchase that changes available money. Design role, not an interviewee.
- Moment: just before paying, financing, or waiting.
- Demo scenario: the phone cash ledger.
- Format: carousel plan.
- CTA: Prueba una compra.
- Claim: the subtraction uses only those demo figures. Class: DEMO.
- Evidence needed: a person’s recorded interpretation. None is attached.
- Risk: a tidy carousel can look like permission to buy.
- Status: concept. The finished carousel is a separate asset that uses this scene.

### C2 — Los audífonos de $1,800

- Hook: Un precio más bajo también cambia el dinero disponible.
- Audience and moment: same as C1.
- Demo scenario: headphones ledger. $4,700 left today.
- Format: caption plan.
- CTA: Prueba una compra.
- Claim: DEMO arithmetic. It does not say the headphones are affordable.
- Evidence needed: a person’s recorded interpretation. None is attached.
- Risk: a smaller price can look harmless.
- Status: concept only. No finished asset uses this scene.

## Series 2 — Lo que realmente comprometes

How price and written payments change available money.

### C3 — El mensual no es el compromiso

- Hook: Ver $1,100 no muestra los doce pagos.
- Demo scenario: written plan totaling $15,200.
- Format: card plan.
- Claim: the total is the sum of the written demo figures. Class: DEMO. No interest rate was invented.
- Evidence needed: none attached.
- Risk: the larger total can look like a warning to refuse the purchase. The card does not say that.
- Status: concept. The share card is the finished file for this scene.

### C4 — Enganche y lo que sigue

- Hook: El enganche es lo que sale hoy. Los pagos escritos siguen después.
- Demo scenario: $2,000 now, $4,500 left today in the example, $13,200 still written for later.
- Format: short note plan.
- Claim: today and later are shown separately. Class: DEMO.
- Evidence needed: none attached.
- Risk: “$4,500 left” can be read as spare money. The coverage lines forbid that reading.
- Status: concept only.

## Series 3 — Comprar ahora vs esperar

Same inputs, two scenarios. No winner.

### C5 — Las dos columnas

- Hook: Mismos datos. Comprar ahora y esperar.
- Demo scenario: −$5,500 if bought now, $6,500 if waiting.
- Format: vertical storyboard plan.
- Claim: both columns come from the same demo inputs. Class: DEMO.
- Evidence needed: none attached.
- Risk: the dissent stands. A visible trade-off may read as permission to buy. The frames say neither column decides.
- Status: concept. The vertical storyboard is the finished file. It is not an MP4.

### C6 — Esperar no es el premio

- Hook: Esperar muestra el dinero que esta compra no usa hoy.
- Demo scenario: waiting is not the prize and buying now is not authorized.
- Format: caption plan.
- Claim: the piece refuses to choose. Class: DEMO.
- Evidence needed: none attached.
- Risk: “esperar” can sound like advice. The wording keeps it as a scenario name.
- Status: concept only.

## What this plan does not contain
