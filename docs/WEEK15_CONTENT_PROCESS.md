# Creative process — PAUSA MX Week 15

Pipeline:

```text
RESEARCH → WRITING → VISUAL PRODUCTION → CLAIM CHECK → STYLE REVIEW → REVISION → HUMAN APPROVAL → PUBLISH/TEST
```

Human approval is required before a piece is treated as published to an audience. Files in the repository and a public page are a release of the product surface. They are not evidence that a person trusted, understood, or acted on the piece.

AI may draft research notes, copy, visuals, claim lists, style checks, and templates.

AI cannot demonstrate trust, resonance, conversion, behavior change, or demand. Those words stay unused as results.

## RESEARCH

- Input: the Week 15 packet, the existing comparison, and public pages for YNAB, NerdWallet, and CONDUSEF fetched on 2026-11-22.
- Output: the audience, the moment, the exclusions, and `docs/WEEK15_BENCHMARKS.md`.
- Quality check: a sentence that is someone else’s published claim is labeled as theirs. A sentence without a source is not upgraded to VERIFIED.

## WRITING

- Input: the official claim, the shadow clause, and the demo table in `docs/WEEK15_PACKET.md`.
- Output: series, six concepts, and the copy inside `src/lib/distribution/content.ts` and `/launch`.
- Quality check: no “sí te alcanza”, no buy-or-don’t-buy verdict, no testimonial, no savings claim, no engagement number.

## VISUAL PRODUCTION

- Input: the approved demo figures and the brand colors already in `src/app/globals.css`.
- Output: SVG files in `public/week15/`, generated by `scripts/build-week15-assets.mjs` so the Spanish sentences stay exact.
- Quality check: each asset shows PAUSA MX, “Datos de demostración”, “Prueba una compra”, and the three coverage lines. The storyboard file is not described as a filmed video.

## CLAIM CHECK

- Input: the written and visual copy.
- Output: each concept carries `claimClass`. Demo pesos are DEMO. The engine’s use of entered numbers is VERIFIED for the product, not for a person’s finances. Audience uptake is UNKNOWN. The workaround list remains ASSUMPTION.
- Quality check: VERIFIED is not used for trust, resonance, conversion, behavior change, or demand.

## STYLE REVIEW

- Input: `docs/WEEK15_BRAND_SYSTEM.md` and the launch page.
- Output: paper background, navy text, teal only as the product action, equal borders on Comprar ahora and Esperar in the launch example.
- Quality check: the voice is calm and direct. A polished frame is not used as financial authority.

## REVISION

- Input: claim check and style review.
- Output: copy changes before the human gate. This pass removed a filmed-video claim from the storyboard and kept both scenarios visually level on `/launch`.
- Quality check: a revision does not invent a quote to justify itself.

## HUMAN APPROVAL

- Input: the piece as it would be shown.
- Output: a person’s yes, no, or change request, dated.
- Quality check: without that note, audience-test and teardown rows stay PENDING HUMAN EVIDENCE. Publishing the URL does not count as approval by an audience.

## PUBLISH/TEST

- Input: the approved piece, or, while approval is pending, a clearly bounded product release that does not claim audience results.
- Output: `/launch` on the public Vercel URL, plus tests of the page mechanics.
- Quality check: a passing test shows that the page renders and the copy is present. It does not show that anyone changed a purchase.
