# Global benchmarks — PAUSA MX Week 15

Fetched 2026-11-22. Figures quoted from these sites are their published claims. They are not PAUSA MX results and they were not re-surveyed.

## YNAB

Pages read:

- https://www.ynab.com/
- https://www.ynab.com/guide/foundations-the-ynab-method
- https://support.ynab.com/en_us/the-ynab-method-an-overview-SJmiqpi6j.md (support page updated 2026-09-03)

What they do well: YNAB teaches a method and then asks the reader to use the app. The homepage moves from “Get YNAB” to a free trial, then to a plan step: ask “What’s it for?” before spending. The guide names the method as “Give every dollar a job.” The support article turns that question into five uses of money: now, later, ease, you, and change.

How content connects to the product: the article is not a separate magazine. It tells the reader to assign money in the plan until Ready to Assign is zero, and it points at Home and Plan in the app.

What their homepage also does, recorded only as their claim: it publishes survey percentages, a TrustScore, review counts, and statements that the average user saves $600 in the first month and $6,000 in the first year, plus named user stories. PAUSA MX does not adopt those numbers or that story form.

What changes for Mexico: the pages reviewed are in English and in dollars. The method is an ongoing budget, not the minute before one discretionary purchase. Account linking and a trial are part of their path. PAUSA MX does not link a bank and does not teach a full budgeting method.

## NerdWallet

Pages and filings read:

- https://www.nerdwallet.com/ on 2026-11-22
- https://www.nerdwallet.com/advertiser-disclosure
- https://www.nerdwallet.com/nerdwallet-editorial-guidelines
- NerdWallet, Inc. Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025, filed February 25, 2026, via the investor site

What they do well: the homepage is a front door to decisions that already have a product category: insurance, mortgages, savings, credit cards. It pairs news with comparison tools. The 10-K describes three platform parts: editorial content, marketplace and referral services, and financial services offered through subsidiaries.

How content connects to the product: an article or a “best of” list sits next to a comparison that can send the reader to a partner. The advertiser disclosure says partners compensate NerdWallet and that this may influence which products appear, while also saying partners cannot buy a favorable editorial review. The 10-K says revenue comes primarily through referral fees, lead generation, and partner-based monetization.

What changes for Mexico: the rates, cards, and lenders on the US homepage are not the Mexican decision in front of a phone or a pair of headphones. CONDUSEF already compares regulated financial products. PAUSA MX is not a marketplace and does not take a referral fee for telling someone to buy.

Their homepage also publishes decision counts, expert counts, app ratings, and review quotes. Those stay NerdWallet’s claims.

## CONDUSEF

Pages read:

- https://www.condusef.gob.mx/
- https://www.condusef.gob.mx/comparativos/
- https://cursoenlinea.condusef.gob.mx/

What they do well: CONDUSEF is the public place in Mexico for complaints, registries of financial institutions, comparative tables, commissions, and free financial-education material. The online course states an objective of helping people make a financial plan, choose and compare financial products, and avoid fraud. Its modules are budget, saving, credit, investment, retirement, insurance, fraud, and CONDUSEF services. The comparative section points at banks, Afores, insurers, credit bureaus, and other sectors.

How content connects to the product: the “product” is public information and a procedure, not a purchase of a phone. A course module leads to concepts and a certificate path. A comparator leads to financial-product terms such as CAT, commissions, and complaints.

What changes for this venture: an adult about to pay for a discretionary good is not, in that moment, choosing among authorized banks. A CONDUSEF course is a class. PAUSA MX is the pause before one payment the person already has in front of them.

## Conclusion

PAUSA MX should not compete as generic financial education.

It should own the moment immediately before a meaningful purchase: one comparison, entered data only, Comprar ahora and Esperar, with the coverage limits visible.

YNAB owns a budgeting method. NerdWallet owns content that feeds a financial-product marketplace. CONDUSEF owns public education and comparison of regulated financial products. None of those jobs is the job of this release.
